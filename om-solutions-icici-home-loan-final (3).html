Eligibility

Who Can Apply?

Home loan eligibility depends on multiple factors including income, age, existing liabilities, credit profile and property details.

  • Applicant's age and repayment capacity
  • Monthly / annual income and employment profile
  • Existing loans and financial obligations
  • Credit history and overall profile
  • Property value and applicable property criteria

👔 Salaried

  • Stable employment and regular income
  • Salary slips / applicable income proof
  • Bank statements and KYC documents

💼 Self Employed

  • Stable business / income profile
  • ITR and applicable financial documents
  • Bank statements and KYC documents
Documents

Keep Your Documents Ready

  • PAN Card and applicable KYC / identity proof
  • Address proof
  • Latest salary slips for salaried applicants
  • Form 16, where applicable
  • ITR / financial documents for self-employed applicants
  • Recent bank statements
  • Relevant property documents
Important

Document Requirements Can Vary

Exact documents depend on the applicant profile, property and lender requirements. The final list is confirmed during the application process.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum income required for a home loan?

Income requirements depend on the applicant's profile, existing obligations, credit history and applicable lender eligibility criteria.

Can self-employed individuals apply?

Eligible self-employed applicants can apply subject to income, documentation, credit and lender criteria.

Can I transfer my existing home loan?

Eligible borrowers may explore home-loan balance-transfer options subject to applicable lender terms and eligibility.

How much home loan can I get?

The eligible amount depends on income, age, existing liabilities, credit profile, property value and repayment capacity.

What documents are required?

KYC, income and property-related documents are generally required. Exact requirements can vary by applicant and property.

Does submitting this form guarantee approval?

No. Submission of an enquiry does not guarantee approval or disbursement. Final decisions are subject to lender verification and applicable terms.
